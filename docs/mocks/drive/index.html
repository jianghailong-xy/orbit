Documents · design board

The account's drive, from docs/drive-product-design.md (decisions D1–D23 and rules 1–8, settled by the owner on 2026-11-19). iPhone is the reference design; web, Mac and Android follow it. Four boards: 01-ios.png (iPhone, 18 frames, which are also the phone layouts), 02-web.png (web console, 8 frames), 03-mac.png (Mac app, 4 frames) and 04-android.png (Android app, 15 frames). All names, sessions, sizes and dates are demo data.

Open points for the owner

#QuestionDrawn as
aWhere Trash and Storage are reachedAs the last rows of the drive's top level on the phone, buttons in the Documents page bar on web, and rows under Documents in the Mac sidebar. Not in the left menu, to keep one entry there.
bDelete without a confirmationMove to Trash asks nothing (Trash keeps everything). Only Delete for good and Empty Trash ask.
cRestoring an earlier versionTwo choices: as the current version (today's file goes to Trash in turn) or as a copy beside it.
dUpload name clashAn upload whose name is taken gets a suffix ("labels 2.csv"), like a restore; nothing is overwritten by an upload.
