Antigravity CLI row: its sign-in should look like its neighbours'

Drawn from the Machines page's real grid (.re-row 137/108/190/172, .re-dup 38px indent) and its light tokens. Left is main today, as in your screenshot; right is the proposal.

Now · main

① The only row whose button is labelled with a provider: it is wider than the others, so its left edge leaves the column of Sign in buttons.
② The Google terms line starts at the card's edge, 38px left of the note above it, at 12px instead of 11.5px.
③ On macOS and Android, a signed-out Antigravity row already says Sign in. They show Sign in with Google only when the CLI runs on an API key. Only the web says it every time.

Proposed

① A signed-out row says Sign in, like every other engine and like macOS and Android. The sign-in panel it opens already says Google and shows the terms.
② The terms line moves in under the note and matches its size, so it reads as part of the row.
③ Unchanged: Sign in with Google stays on the row of a CLI that runs on a Gemini API key. There, it tells the two ways of signing in apart, as it does on the native apps.
