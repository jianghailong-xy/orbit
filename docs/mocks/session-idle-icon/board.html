Session list: what the Idle icon should be

Today an idle session wears an empty grey circle, the same shape as an unticked to-do checkbox (Reminders, Things, Linear) and as Orbit's own pickers (circle → checkmark.circle.fill). Most sessions are idle, so the leftmost column is a stack of checkboxes that invite a click and do nothing. Same eight sample rows in each column. The first column is what rows drew before the three-states change of 11/25 (web StatusIcon, Mac SessionStatusGlyph); the other three differ only in the Idle icon. The Queued clock and the blue Running spinner stay as they are.

Before (until 11/25)
  • The idle icon was a speech bubble: nothing about it said "tick me".
  • Each row's icon told you something (reply, approval, failed, done).
  • Five of the nine icons are circles (pause, check, cross, minus): the ended rows' grey ✓ circle is itself checkbox-like, and reads as "done".
  • The bubble said "Waiting for your reply" on every parked conversation, even ones that asked nothing; the three-states work removed that claim.
  • Colour and shape carried state and outcome at once; the mark chips now say outcome in words.
Today
  • Reads as "tick me to finish": the most repeated shape in the list is a false affordance.
  • The one Running row is one icon among eight; it has to be searched for.
A · Nothing for Idle (recommended)
  • Nothing to click by mistake; the column only lights up when something is happening.
  • Running and Queued are found at a glance: they are the only icons.
  • Nothing is lost: the header still says Idle; Failed, Finished, Needs you, Ended are marks.
  • The slot keeps its width, so titles stay aligned.
  • Phones drew nothing for Idle before the three-states board, so this goes back to what they knew.
B · Small grey dot
  • Reads as a status dot, not a checkbox.
  • Still one mark on every row: noise that says nothing.
  • A dot next to coloured dots elsewhere (presence, unread) invites "what colour does it turn?".
