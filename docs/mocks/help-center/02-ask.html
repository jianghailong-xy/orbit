帮助中心 · 02 · Ask：问一个读过文档的 agent

搜不到、或者不知道该搜什么时，用 Ask。它是一个普通的 session，跑在你自己的一台机器、一个 engine 上（和 Wiki 聊天一样：工具、费用、手机上能看，全都照旧），只是有三条限制：
① 只能读：帮助文章 + 你账户的只读状态（机器在不在线、任务卡在哪），不能改任何东西；② 每个结论引用文章；③ 要改设置时给一个按钮，由你点。

Why is build-box offline?
✓ Read 2 articles · ✓ Checked build-box1
build-box last checked in 2 h 4 min ago, right after its last session finished, and Orbit has heard nothing since. That pattern usually means the computer went to sleep, not that the runner crashed 1.
  1. Wake build-box, or check it is powered on.
  2. If it doesn't come back in a minute, run orbit doctor on it 1.
  3. To stop this happening, turn off sleep while plugged in 2.
Its two paused sessions resume on their own once it is back.
