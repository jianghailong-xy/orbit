Orbit's own turns · five that still draw as raw blocks

After the You confirmed card, these turns are the rest of what Orbit writes for an agent and the clients still draw as the reader's bubble or as an empty bubble with the block folded under it. Each gets a card built like the three confirmation-review cards: what happened in one line, links instead of ids, local time, and the agent's block folded at the foot.

规则

卡片颜色读自原文折叠标题

Notice from a task run灰RUN_NOTIFY 请求（任务、原话、状态）What the coordinator was told
Orbit started this session…灰色一行会话的 prompt_by_orbit + 它是哪种会话（项目协调者 / 判定 / 清单派发 / 提及回复）Show brief

共同规则：不是「我」的气泡，不进可放回输入框的「我的话」；排队虚线 + Cancel；未确认送达一句橙字；时间一律本地；状态句随行的当前状态现算（截止已过、已判、已答、被取代）；重发沿用原卡片。
