What goes wrong with the text
  1. Settled cards keep their full shell. Each "Sent back to the coordinator" card still draws "Escalated to you" + FROM ORBIT, three lines for a closed item, stacked above the one that needs you.
  2. Three heading lines before the point. "Escalated to you", "FROM ORBIT", then "Now yours — …". The actual cause sits in body-size text under two labels.
  3. The sentence is the loudest thing on screen. ~21pt bold over 3 lines, louder than the heading, and it repeats what the buttons say.
  4. Doors read as disabled text. "Retry", "Open landing", "Mark as handled" are centred grey words, while their captions are left-aligned. They don't look tappable, and the column doesn't line up.
  5. Raw facts wrap freely. A long Chinese task title takes 2 lines. The check command wraps over 3 mono lines and ends in "…" with a separate "Show details".
  6. Output box shows the wrong part. A fixed grey box shows the tail ("}" and "Failure lines"), and the floating bottom bar covers its last line. The failing test names are below the fold.

The rules
  1. Settled items fold into one row: "✓ 2 sent back to the coordinator · Show". Nothing settled sits above an open item at full size.
  2. One heading line: a small "⚠ Escalated to you" label with the clock on the right, then the cause in 17pt semibold. "FROM ORBIT" moves to the bottom, as on the board.
  3. No giant sentence. The required-action sentence isn't drawn on the new card. "Who fixes it?" plus the green closing line say the same thing in 13pt.
  4. Every door is a real button, left-aligned. Primary is filled, secondary is tinted, links are blue. "Retry…" ends in an ellipsis because it asks for a reason first (task 34ycmMcm).
  5. Facts are clamped. The subline is one line with a tail ellipsis, and the full title lives in Details. The command is one line with "Copy command" instead of wrapping.
  6. Output shows the failing lines, not the tail. Details lists the ✖ lines (up to 3) in a white box, then "Full output (N lines)". The scroll view keeps a bottom inset so the floating bar never covers content.
