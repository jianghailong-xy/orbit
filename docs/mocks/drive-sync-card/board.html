Documents sync: a card in the conversation

When an agent calls documents_sync, the conversation shows one sync card where the call was made. It carries the same live progress as the Documents page's tray, and it keeps updating after the agent's turn ends, because the sync runs in the background. When the sync ends, the card folds to one line, and the agent's next turn (its wake) replies with the summary. It is the transfer card's family (documents_put / documents_get): same header, bar and stats line, folded the same way when done. All copy in English; names, sizes and times are demo data.

① Where it sits: the call, the turn that ends, and the wake

What this decides

  1. One card per documents_sync call, in place of its tool-call line. It is never grouped with other tools: a sync is not one of many quick calls.
  2. It outlives the turn. The transfer card is live only while its tool call runs; the sync card is live until the sync ends, however many turns later. The conversation page reads it from the server like the tray, so it is right after a reload and on another device.
  3. The wake reply stays the agent's own words. The card holds the numbers; the reply says what matters.
  4. Background chip: says it goes on without the agent. Open folder opens the destination in Documents.
