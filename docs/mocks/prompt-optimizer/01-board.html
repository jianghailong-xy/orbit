Prompt optimizer: DeepSeek improves a draft before it is sent

Off by default. With Settings › General › Prompt optimizer on, the composer gets an ✦ Optimize button. It sends the draft and the session's context to the server, which asks DeepSeek (the server's own DEEPSEEK_API_KEY, as Translation and Suggested replies do) for a clearer, more complete prompt. The result is shown as a suggestion; nothing goes into the input box until the owner presses Use, and nothing is sent until they press Send. Hand-drawn from index.css tokens and the real composer class names; the classes marked NEW do not exist yet. iOS, macOS and Android draw the same states (their frames follow once this board is agreed).

Open decisions

D1 · What "project context" is sent to DeepSeek

A. The draft plus what the server already holds: the session's recap, its last few turns (bounded, ~8k tokens, the recap's budget), the Orbit project's goal and acceptance when the session belongs to one, the workspace name, and wiki entries that match the draft. One DeepSeek call, no runner round-trip.

B. A plus files from the checkout (README, CLAUDE.md, files the draft names), read through the runner. Richer, but it needs a new runner route and is slower, and it does nothing while the runner is offline.

C. The draft and the recap only. Cheapest, but it mostly rephrases.

D2 · Language when Translation is also on

A. The optimizer replies in English and the turn is marked already-English, so Send skips the translation: one DeepSeek call in total, as you chose earlier. The card shows the English with "Show in Chinese" for reading.

B. The optimizer replies in the owner's language and Send translates it as today: two calls, but the owner reviews and edits in their own language.

D3 · How the suggestion is drawn

A. The card above the composer (frame 4), additions tinted, the draft untouched until Use.

B. A side-by-side diff of draft and suggestion. More precise, too wide for a phone.

Settled by your answers
  • Composer button, preview first; the input box changes only after the owner confirms (Use).
  • Off by default (an account preference promptOptimizer, absent = off); unavailable without the server's DeepSeek key.
  • Every client: server, web, iOS/macOS, Android. The phone draws the card as a sheet over the composer, with the same three actions.
