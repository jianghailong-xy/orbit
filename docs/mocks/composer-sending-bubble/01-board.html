To decide

Ⓐ While one message is sending, can you send the next?

  1. A · Type yes, send waits (recommended). The box is free, and Send lights up once the first message settles, usually in well under a second. No ordering problem, and nothing new on the server.
  2. B · Send it too. The second bubble waits behind the first and goes when it settles. This needs a small client-side queue, and a failure of the first one would have to hold the second.

Ⓑ How long before "Not sent"?

  1. A · 20 seconds (recommended). A send normally comes back in under a second. A new session (create) can take a few seconds. 20 s leaves room for both and still tells you before you give up.
  2. B · 60 seconds. Fewer false "Not sent" lines on a very slow link, but a long time to look at "Sending…".

Either way, a late success after the line appears turns the bubble back into a normal sent message. Nothing is sent twice, because Retry reuses the same clientTurnId.
