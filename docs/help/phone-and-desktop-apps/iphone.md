The iPhone and iPad app shows the same account as the web: your projects, tasks, sessions and wiki. Agents still run on your machines; the phone is where you follow them and answer.

## Install it

The app is distributed through TestFlight: install TestFlight from the App Store, open your Orbit invitation in it, and install Orbit. Each Orbit release ships a new build there.

## Sign in

1. Open the app. It says **Welcome back**, "Sign in to continue to Orbit".
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Sign In**, or tap **Continue with Google** if your server offers it.

::: platform ios
**A self-hosted server.** The server address is hidden until you need it: triple-tap the app icon on the sign-in screen (VoiceOver users: the **Change server** action). In **Server**, type the **Server address** your administrator gave you and tap **Save**. **Reset to** the default puts it back.
:::

## What is where

- The drawer leads with **Projects**, **Tasks** and **Wiki**, then **Following**, **Workspaces** and the rest.
- **Settings** starts with **Machines & models**: "Where your agents run, and whose model quota they spend." Below it are **Preferences** (Sessions, Notifications, Appearance) and **Account** (Email, Instance, Shared links, Access tokens, Change password, and Admin for administrators).

## Approvals and questions

Approval cards and confirmations work as on the web: they appear in the session that asked, and its row says what it wants, such as **Decision for you**. Open the session and answer. A terminal asking to sign in shows a one-time **Approve a terminal** toast and waits on its machine's page under **Machines & models**. See [Find what needs you](orbit:/help/projects-and-tasks/find-what-needs-you). Turn on [notifications](orbit:/help/phone-and-desktop-apps/notifications) so you hear about them.
