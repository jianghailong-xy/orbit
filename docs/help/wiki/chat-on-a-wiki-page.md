Every wiki page has a chat. Use it to ask what a page means, or to say what is wrong or missing.

## Open the chat

Press the round **Chat** button at the bottom right of the page. The panel opens beside the page, so you can read while you talk.

- Type a question, or say what should change, and press **Ask**.
- **New chat** starts over.

The chat is an agent session like any other, and its cost counts like any session's. It runs in one of your workspaces for this repository; **Runs in** at the top of the panel says which. If none is set up, the panel says what to turn on. When that machine is offline, the chat waits for it to come back.

## Changes wait for you

The agent can draft a change to the page, but "Nothing in the wiki changes until you apply it." Read its draft, then apply it or ask for another try.

## What the button tells you

While the chat is closed, the button says what it is doing:

- **Working…**: the agent is still answering.
- **1 needs you** (or **2 need you**): a draft or a question is waiting for you.
- **Wiki updated**: the wiki changed after something you applied.
