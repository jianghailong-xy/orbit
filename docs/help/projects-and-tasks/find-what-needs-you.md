There is no separate inbox. Whatever an agent needs from you waits where the work is: in the session that asked, on a machine's page, or on the Scheduled page.

## Requests from a session

When a session asks you for something, its card appears in that session's conversation. In the session list, its row gets an amber dot and a word that says what it wants:

- **Decision for you**: choose an option. The card shows the one the agent recommends.
- **Key needed**: provide a value only you have, such as a token.
- **Steps for you**: something only a person can do, listed step by step.
- **Confirm for you**: a task is done and needs you to say so. See [Evidence and confirmation](orbit:/help/projects-and-tasks/evidence-and-confirmation).
- **Approve N task moves**: the session wants to move tasks from one project to another.

A session that had completed returns to **Open** while one of its requests is unanswered, so it never hides in the completed ones. Links and notification buttons open the session with its card in view.

## Moving tasks between projects

A request to move tasks into another project is approved on one card. The card shows in the target project's coordinator session and in the session that asked. Answer it in either place; the other settles too.

## Terminals asking to sign in

A terminal asking to act as you waits on its machine's page, under **Settings → Machines**, in **Terminals to approve**. On the web and on the Mac, the session list shows a **Needs you** line that opens that page. On iPhone, iPad and Android, a new request shows a one-time **Approve a terminal** toast, and its notification opens the machine page. Approved terminals are listed on each machine's page, where **Revoke** signs one out.

## Scheduled tasks

A scheduled run that failed, was held or stopped for your approval waits on the [Scheduled](orbit:/scheduled) page and in that run's session.

## In a project

Questions from the coordinator and the confirmations it needs also appear at the top of the project page, under **Needs you**.

## Hear about them

Turn on notifications to hear about new requests without keeping Orbit open. See [Notifications](orbit:/help/phone-and-desktop-apps/notifications).
