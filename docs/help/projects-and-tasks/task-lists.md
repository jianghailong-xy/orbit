[Tasks](orbit:/tasks) shows the tasks that are not part of a project. Tasks in projects live on their project's page; the note under the title links there.

## Choose what to show

The page title is a menu: **All tasks**, **No list**, or one of your lists. Lists are grouped under **Task List** and, once finished, **Completed**.

A list is a set of related tasks that one session steers. Lists are made by agents: ask a session to organise work into a list, and it shows you a card first. On a list's page, **Steering session ›** opens the conversation the list is steered from.

## Filter and search

- Tabs with counts: **All**, **Open**, **Ready**, **Running**, **Failed**, **Done**.
- **Tasks** / **Batches** switches between single tasks and the batches they were created in.
- **Search tasks** and **Labels** narrow the list further.

## Act on several at once

Select tasks to get the bulk bar: **Run**, **Stop**, **Set assignee**, **Delete**, and **Clear** to drop the selection.

## A task's own page

Open a task to see its description, its definition of done, the sessions that ran it, its evidence and its comments.
