[Waiting on you](orbit:/waiting) collects everything an agent has asked you to do, so work does not stall unseen. Its count in the sidebar is how many items are open.

## What appears here

- **Decisions**: an agent or a coordinator asks you to choose. Press **Answer**.
- **Secrets**: an agent needs a value only you have, such as a token. Press **Provide**.
- **Manual steps**: something only a person can do, listed step by step. Press **Open steps**.
- **Confirmations**: a task is done and needs you to say so. Press **Review**. See [Evidence and confirmation](orbit:/help/projects-and-tasks/evidence-and-confirmation).
- **Terminal approvals**: a terminal asking to act as you is shown in full at the top.

Each row says what it holds, for example how many tasks wait on it, or the option the agent recommends.

## Tabs

- **Open**: still waiting. "Nothing is waiting on you." means you are clear.
- **Done**: what you answered.
- **Declined**: what you turned down.

## On your phone

Turn on notifications to hear about new items without keeping Orbit open. See [Notifications](orbit:/help/phone-and-desktop-apps/notifications).
