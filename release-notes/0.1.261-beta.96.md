# Orbit 0.1.261-beta.96

## Summary

This release is mostly what an owner sees on the web: the session page gets a one-row header, its settings open in a column beside the conversation, and the top pill absorbs the question bar and the jump to your last message. A project's title and acceptance criteria are now translated for the owner on the done cards and the project page on web, macOS, iPhone and Android; session rows show the time of their last message instead of when they were listed; and the Apple Agents sheet draws each row in one two-line shape.

- **Web: a one-row session header.** On a wide window the header is the title alone — no status line, tags, Follow chip or rule under it — with the conversation fading out at the top (docs/mocks/session-header-compact, revision 4). Share moves out of ⋯ onto its own button, green while a link is live, and Follow… moves into ⋯; a handoff in flight still shows Handing over beside the title. Phones keep their header.

- **Web: one control at the top of the transcript.** A wide window draws the last-message pill in the header row beside Share and ⋯, centred, and a long title truncates before running under it; a phone still floats the pill over the transcript. The sticky question bar folded into it, so the question the bar named is the pill's main press, with the jump to the beginning beside it, and the bar, its CSS and its tests are gone. When the reader has left the tail, the pill offers the owner's own last message even over a run Orbit opened, loading older pages one at a time (30 at most) until it finds one. The "Loading earlier messages…" pill now shows its loading state in the header pill rather than as a second bubble over the transcript.

- **Web: session settings beside the conversation.** Clicking the session title opens its settings in the project column's slot, one column at a time, instead of a drawer that covered and dimmed the conversation (docs/mocks/session-settings-column). The session is renamed in the new Name field and the double-click rename is gone; a phone keeps the full-screen sheet. The open title has no look of its own, just its hover and keyboard-focus ground and ›.

- **Project title and criteria, translated for the owner.** With Settings › Translation on, `GET /projects/:id` serves `translation` (ProjectTextTranslation) in the owner's language, kept in the new `project_text_translation` table (migration 1600) with a digest of the words it was made from, so an edit, another language or translation off serves none and queues a new one (decision 1A). Web, macOS, iPhone and Android draw that translation on "Is this project done?" (asked or not), its receipt, the Done when list, the why-not-done card and the project page's criteria list, with one Translated · Show original foot; writes and the dialogs they open keep the document's own words.

- **Session rows date by their last message.** A wake, session-to-session or background-job turn left `listedAt` where it was, so a row's preview showed a new reply beside "1d ago". The owner chose (2026-11-25) to show `lastTurnAt` on web, Apple and Android while the order and date sections stay on `listedAt`; web's project row now takes `lastTurnAt` from the member that spoke last, as Apple and Android already did.

- **Apple: Agents sheet rows in one two-line shape.** Each row is now the title with its time on the right, then the engine mark, the model (no build number) and the other machine on one line, and a running row adds its live time and current step (docs/mocks/agents-sheet-rows), instead of wrapping to two or three lines and repeating a long tinted model chip.

- **Runner: the old Drive links are removed.** The runner's cleanup only matched links to the current mount, `/run/orbit-drive/drive`, so an Orbit Cloud home from the Drive rollout kept `~/Documents` -> `/run/orbit-documents/drive` and `~/Files` -> `/run/orbit-files/drive`; it now removes those links too, and the owner does not want either directory.

## Upgrade impact

- **Migrations:** one, applied by the apiserver on boot.
  - `1600_project_text_translation` adds one table, `project_text_translation`: one row per project keyed by its id, with `owner_id`, a `JSONB` `translation`, an `updated_at` defaulted to the current time, and a foreign key to `project` that cascades on delete. **Additive**: no existing table, column or row is touched, nothing is backfilled, and `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` lets it run twice. The `CREATE TABLE` takes a SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE lock on `project` for the foreign key only, 2 s at a time, 1 s apart, up to 60 tries; no INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE.

- **Configuration:** none. No environment variable or setting changes in this release.

- **Server:** the only wire change is the new `translation` field on `GET /projects/:id`; no existing field changes. A project is translated the first time it is read with translation on, so until that translation is written the page and cards show the original words. There is no backfill and no one-time database step.

- **Runners:** the runner served at `/dl` is built from this tag. It now removes a `~/Documents` or `~/Files` link that points at an older Drive driver's mount. No other runner change; a runner older than this release keeps working.

- **Clients:** web arrives with the deploy of this commit; macOS (beta channel DMG), iOS (TestFlight) and Android (APK) come from this tag. Web takes the one-row session header, the settings column and the merged top pill, the translated project title and criteria, and the last-message row date. macOS and the iPhone take the translated project title and criteria, the Agents sheet rows and the last-message row date. Android takes the translated project title and criteria and the last-message row date. No one-time step on a Mac, iPhone or Android device.

## Security fixes

None in this release.

## Known limitations

- A project's translation is served only while Settings › Translation is on, the language matches and the source digest is current. Editing the title or a criterion leaves the row stale: the old translation is not served and the next read queues a new one, so the original words show until it is written.
- Nothing is backfilled, so a project first read with translation on shows its original title and criteria until its translation lands.
- The one-row session header, the settings-in-a-column and the merged top pill are web changes on a wide window; a phone keeps the older header, the full-screen settings sheet and the floating pill.
- A runner older than this release does not remove the old `~/Documents` and `~/Files` Drive links, so an Orbit Cloud home keeps them until a runner from this release runs.
