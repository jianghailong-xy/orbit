# Orbit 0.1.261-beta.3

## Summary

The work merged since [0.1.261-beta.2](0.1.261-beta.2.md), on the beta channel.

- **The needs-you capsule floats** (iOS and Android). It sits over the Sessions list and the
  conversation rather than taking a strip of its own: the rows scroll on behind it, and at rest the first
  row and the pull-to-refresh spinner stay clear of it. When several things are waiting, a deck under the
  capsule says so (it replaces the "+N ⌄" pill). Unfolded, the capsules hang over the page on a dimmed
  scrim, and a tap on the scrim folds them, instead of pushing the page down.
- **An approval arriving with the app open is the capsule** (iOS). For six seconds the capsule carries
  the push's own line with an amber ring, and a tap goes straight to that session; then it goes back to
  the count. The separate amber approval card is gone. Pages with no capsule of their own (Tasks,
  Projects, Wiki, pages pushed over a list) now show the same capsule under their navigation bar.
- **New wiki…**: an agent can ask the owner to create a wiki with a `WIKI_CREATE` owner card, and web,
  Apple and Android have New wiki… entry points. `POST /wiki/spaces` is owner-only and refuses a second
  wiki for the same repository.
- **SubAgent**: a Codex account pool can now be a SubAgent's routed pick and fallback target, and the
  SubAgent block's Why line on iOS and Android is the server's one-line why, as on web.
- **CI**: a macOS runtime crash gate that clicks the sidebar 200 times.

## Upgrade impact

- **Runners.** Runners on the beta channel move to 0.1.261-beta.3; the runner now renders wiki search
  being unavailable and describes `WIKI_CREATE` in `request_owner_action`. A runner on 0.1.261-beta.2
  keeps working.
- **Clients.** macOS users who enabled "Receive beta updates" get this build. iOS ships to TestFlight
  (marketing 0.1.261, build number = commit count). Android offers the APK to every install via
  `android-update.json`. On iOS, an approval that arrives in the foreground no longer shows a toast;
  the needs-you capsule says it.
- **Database migrations.** Added since 0.1.261-beta.2: `0531_owner_request_wiki_create` (two nullable
  columns on `owner_request`, `WIKI_CREATE` added to its kind check, a partial unique index) and
  `0800_pool_login_token_subagent_credential` (two nullable columns on `pool_login_token`). Applied by
  the apiserver on boot.
- **Configuration.** No changes required.

## Security fixes

None in this release.

## Known limitations

- Android still shows an approval that arrives in the foreground as its bottom snackbar; only iOS moved
  that into the needs-you capsule.
- The known limitations of [0.1.261-beta.2](0.1.261-beta.2.md) still apply, except the two SubAgent
  follow-ups (the Why line and Codex pools) that this beta closes.

## Artifacts and verification

The release identity is `package.json` `0.1.261-beta.3` and tag `v0.1.261-beta.3`; `release.yml`'s
identity job refuses a tag whose name and package version disagree or whose notes are missing. This tag
publishes the macOS DMG and Sparkle payload (beta channel), the iOS TestFlight build, the runner
reference binaries, and `orbit-android-0.1.261-beta.3.apk` with its `.sha256` and
`android-update.json` on the same GitHub Release. The needs-you change passed the macOS build, the iOS
build and Android CI (test, lint, debug APK) on its rebased tree; OrbitKit's only failures were 21
text-parity checks against web files that were already failing before it.

## Rollback

Roll back to 0.1.261-beta.2. The migrations above only add nullable columns, a check value and an index,
so an application-only rollback is schema-safe; `WIKI_CREATE` requests created in the meantime are
simply not shown by the older build. Runners do not move back by themselves: roll them back with the
`/api/runner/release` pointer. See
[Release process](../docs/release-process.md#rollback-decision-and-procedure).
