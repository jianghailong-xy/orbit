# Orbit 0.1.261-beta.8

## Summary

The work merged since [0.1.261-beta.7](0.1.261-beta.7.md), on the beta channel. Client-only.

- **The needs-you card is a notification's size, and moves (iOS and Android).** The card over the
  session list and other pages is now about 70pt with 24pt corners, a 38pt amber mark and two 15pt lines,
  like an iOS notification (it was a 44pt capsule). Unfolding several, the cards after the first slide out
  from under it one after another and fold back the same way, over a dimmed backdrop; the card drops in
  from behind the nav bar and lifts away; a swipe up sends it off shrinking, then the list eases up after
  it; an arriving approval's amber ring pulses once (iOS); the conversation's nav capsule changes between
  the question and what waits with the words pushed through. With Reduce Motion on, iOS fades instead.
- **Fixed: the card over the iPhone session list was squeezed to its mark and an ellipsis** in beta.6 and
  beta.7. It now spans the list.
- **Fixed (iOS): the phone's context sheet draws its rows through one `ForEach`,** the List row shape the
  app uses everywhere else to avoid SwiftUI's List crash.

## Upgrade impact

- **Database migrations.** None.
- **Runners.** Runners on the beta channel move to 0.1.261-beta.8; nothing in the runner changed.
- **Configuration.** No changes required.
- **Clients.** macOS users who enabled "Receive beta updates" get this build. iOS ships to TestFlight
  (marketing version 0.1.261, build number = commit count). Android offers the APK to every install via
  `android-update.json`.

## Security fixes

None in this release.

## Known limitations

- Android has no Reduce Motion fallback for the card's motion, and still shows an approval that arrives
  in the foreground as its bottom snackbar rather than in the card.
- The known limitations of [0.1.261-beta.7](0.1.261-beta.7.md) still apply.

## Artifacts and verification

The release identity is `package.json` `0.1.261-beta.8` and tag `v0.1.261-beta.8`. This tag publishes the
macOS DMG and Sparkle payload (beta channel), the iOS TestFlight build, the runner binaries, and
`orbit-android-0.1.261-beta.8.apk` with its `.sha256` and `android-update.json` on the same GitHub Release.

The needs-you change was checked on its rebased tree: the macOS and iOS builds, OrbitKit (5304 tests,
0 failures) and Android CI (test, lint, debug APK); before the rebase, iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator shots
of the list with the card folded and unfolded, light and dark (card 400x70, the second unfolded card 8pt
below the first), and the Android debug unit suite (2789 tests, 0 failures).

## Rollback

Roll back to 0.1.261-beta.7. There are no migrations, so an application-only rollback is safe. Runners
do not move back by themselves: roll them back with the `/api/runner/release` pointer. See
[Release process](../docs/release-process.md#rollback-decision-and-procedure).
